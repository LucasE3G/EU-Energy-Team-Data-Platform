# What rose and fell in each country during its 2026 heatwaves

Change in average output between all 2026 heatwave days and all non-heatwave days of
the same warm season (May-September), weekday matched to weekday and weekend to weekend.
Figures are power. "Net trade" is the change in net imports: positive means the country
kept more power at home, either by importing more or by exporting less.

Countries with fewer than 10 heatwave days in 2026 are excluded, as is the Netherlands,
whose reported load steps down 4.3 GW on 21 July 2026 and cannot be compared across that break.
